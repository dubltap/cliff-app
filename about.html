What Is CLIFF NOTEZ?

CLIFF NOTEZ is a free AI research assistant built on an evidence-first philosophy. In a world flooded with confident-but-wrong AI outputs, CLIFF NOTEZ takes a different approach: every claim is rated for confidence, every assertion is traced to a source, and the output is designed to tell you not just what is likely true, but how certain we are about it.

Think of it as having a PhD-level research partner available 24/7. You bring the question. CLIFF NOTEZ brings the rigor — multi-source verification, confidence scoring, and synthesis that goes 10 levels deeper than a Wikipedia skim.

"From mystery to mastery." That's the CLIFF NOTEZ promise. Whatever you're trying to understand — a scientific concept, a historical event, a market trend, a technical domain — we'll take you from 'I don't know' to 'I genuinely understand this' as fast as possible.

How It Works

  1. Ask your research question

    Type any topic, claim, or question. The more specific you are, the sharper the analysis — but broad questions work too. CLIFF NOTEZ knows how to scope.

  2. Receive multi-source verified analysis

    The AI synthesizes information across sources, assigns confidence ratings to individual claims, and structures the output in a format you can read, understand, and cite.

  3. Go deeper or follow the threads

    Every CLIFF NOTEZ response is a starting point, not an endpoint. Ask follow-up questions, challenge claims, or request a deeper dive into any sub-topic.
